Nothing is sent for you

No application is ever submitted on your behalf. Open portal takes you to the employer's own form, and you fill in the last step. The letters and resumes are drafts prepared for you to check first.

Where the words come from

Every letter is built only from what's in your resume. If a figure isn't in there, it can't appear in a letter — but read them anyway. They're a strong first draft, not a finished thing.
